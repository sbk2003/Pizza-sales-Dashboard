# Pizza-sales-Dashboard

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/634be414-b8bf-43d9-80c3-a8b69e3f812d)

Total Sales: In the analyzed period, Pizza Hut generated $817,900 in revenue from 21,000 orders.
Average Order Value: The average order value is $38.31.
Pizza Sales: A total of 50,000 pizzas were sold, with an average of 2.32 pizzas per order.
Popular Pizzas: Classic pizzas were the most popular, followed by Supreme and Veggie.
Sales Trends: Daily sales peaked on Friday, while monthly sales showed a decline from March to June, followed by an increase in the second half of the year.
